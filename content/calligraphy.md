# Calligraphy
I love broad-edge calligraphy. I own a Pilot Parallel and fill it with schoolkid Pilot V5/V7 ink. I use my spare record notebooks for paper. Doing this has bled into my normal handwriting,
giving me an ornamental style.

So far I've practiced 3 "types" of broad-edge calligraphy:
## Latin
Over practice, I've developed my own style.
### Styles
Fraktur, Blackletter, Gothic
### Sources
Medieval Western European manuscripts and Bibles from images I found online

## Perso-Arabic
I don't know this very well, but it looks good
### Styles
Naskh, Nastaliq
### Sources
Islamic/Quranic calligraphy from images I found online

## Brahmi
### Styles
Devanagari, Ranjana
### Sources
Devanagari: Posters, lorry signage in Mumbai, Vedic inscriptions from images I found online
Ranjana: Images I found online
